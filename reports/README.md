# Executive & Technical Reports

This directory stores generated analytical summaries, model evaluation scorecards, and curtailment economic impact reports.

## Key Research & Technical Reports

1. **Backtesting Scorecard (`model_performance_scorecard.json`)**:
   - Comprehensive comparative metrics across baseline persistence, Prophet alone, LSTM alone, and the Stacked Ensemble.
   - Breakdown of classification metrics (Precision, Recall, F1, PR-AUC) on extreme curtailment event detection (> 1,000 MW).

2. **Economic Curtailment Analysis**:
   - Quantifying renewable energy waste across CAISO SP15 and NP26 zones.
   - Analysis of negative pricing hours in CAISO day-ahead and real-time wholesale markets.
   - Evaluation of battery storage (BESS) dispatch strategies to absorb anticipated curtailment volume.

3. **Transmission Congestion & Grid Stress Synthesis**:
   - Correlating path 15/26 transmission bottlenecks with localized solar farm curtailment.
   - Ramifications of Spring hydro runoff coinciding with solar peaks.
